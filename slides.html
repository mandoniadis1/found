Madison Andoniadis · MGT 409 final project · gpt-6-luna

The problem

  • Yale's startup scene is spread over separate sites: 122 startups on the Pitchbook directory, 130 opportunities on the Tsai CITY board.
  • One filter at a time, 5 pages of listings, founders hidden.
  • Listings are self-reported: 16 of 97 are misleading.
  • Founders want local support before seed.
  • As co-lead of the Startup & Entrepreneurship Club: a known gap that needs a fix.

What's new

1place for both Yale sources, multi-filter searchvs Pitchbook + Tsai CITY separately
✓verified people: Yale email, proven founder claims, and a way in for people who want to join a startupvs one-way directories
AIthat can't invent, and an Auditor that fixes the datavs ChatGPT guessing

Compared: Yale Pitchbook, Tsai CITY board, Harmonic, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, ChatGPT

Two small agents, lots of plain code

Scout: ask in plain English

  • One luna call turns the question into filters.
  • Code searches and writes the sentence, so nothing is invented.
  • 587 tokens, 1.75 s, $0.00007 a question.
  • 19/20 test questions fully correct.

The Listing Auditor fixes the directory

  • Reads the startup's own website (visible text only).
  • Suggests a fix with quotes verified in code.
  • A person clicks Apply; the fix survives data refreshes.
  • 16 misleading listings found in 5 min for $0.03.
